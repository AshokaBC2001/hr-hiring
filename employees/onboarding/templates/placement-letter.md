# Placement letter

The wording of the placement letter. Change it here; no code needs to change.

Blanks that code fills in:
    {name}        the candidate's full name
    {first_name}  the candidate's first name
    {area}        the area they are placed in
    {work_id}     their work ID
    {start_date}  start date: the Monday after the interview, for example 30 November 2026
    {end_date}    end date: six months later, for example 29 May 2027
                  (the HR officer can change both dates on the candidate page)
    {details}     the table of Work ID, Area, Start date and End date (on a line of its own)

The first line after the line of dashes is the subject. Paragraphs are separated by an empty line.
The date, reference, address block and closing ("Yours sincerely," and the department) are added by code.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
Subject: Placement Letter

Dear {name},

We are pleased to confirm your placement in the {area} area, following your interview.

{details}

Please bring this letter and your national identity card on your first day.

We look forward to working with you.
